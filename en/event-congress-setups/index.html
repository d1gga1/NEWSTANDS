Service 03 / 08

Event and congress setups

Stages, set designs, plenary halls, corporate conventions and product launches: we build events and congresses in Milan and across Europe with the precision of a trade-fair build.

Message us on WhatsAppRequest a quote →

Milan is one of Europe's capitals for events and congresses, with venues such as Allianz MiCo and the city's major corporate locations. Working from the Milan area, we know the timings, rules and logistics of these venues and can handle site visits and installations very quickly.

We design the complete setup: stage, set design, plenary hall, exhibition areas, reception, signage and lighting. We coordinate all the technical suppliers ourselves, so you have one point of contact for the entire event.

What's included

  1. 01

    Stages and set design

    Custom stages, backdrops, LED walls and sets designed around your message.

  2. 02

    Plenary halls and congresses

    Fit-out of halls, poster areas, welcome desks and sponsor spaces.

  3. 03

    Conventions and product launches

    Settings that present a product or bring your sales network together in a memorable way.

  4. 04

    Audio, video and lighting

    Technical production, truss, LED walls and lighting built into the project.

  5. 05

    Signage and graphics

    Wayfinding, totems, backdrops and coordinated graphics, printed and installed by us.

Why NEWSTANDS

  • Based in Milan

    We work from the Milan area, just a short drive from Fiera Milano Rho and Allianz MiCo: quick site visits, simple logistics and crews always close to the major exhibition centres.

  • In-house production

    Structures, wall panels, graphics, furniture and lighting are all made in our own workshop: fewer hand-offs, fewer surprises, deadlines met.

  • At every exhibition centre in Europe

    Our installation crews travel with your stand: Germany, France, Spain, Benelux, Austria, Switzerland and any other venue on request.

  • One point of contact

    From briefing to dismantling you always deal with the same person, plus a dedicated site manager at the show.

Frequently asked questions

Do you also set up events outside exhibition centres?

Yes: hotels, congress centres, industrial spaces and private venues, in Milan, elsewhere in Italy and abroad.

Can you also handle the audio and video side?

Yes, we coordinate stage, LED walls, lighting and audio as part of a single project, with one point of contact.

How far in advance should an event be organised?

The earlier the better: with a few weeks' notice we can carry out the site visit, design and production without rushing. For urgent requests, contact us and we'll assess feasibility.

Got a trade fair coming up?

Tell us the trade fair, city, dates and floor space: we'll get back to you with an initial proposal and a clear quote.
