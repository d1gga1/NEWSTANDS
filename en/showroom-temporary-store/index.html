Service 04 / 08

Showrooms, temporary stores and pop-ups

Temporary retail spaces, pop-ups and permanent showrooms, fitted out with the same rigour as a trade-fair build, in Milan and across Europe.

Message us on WhatsAppRequest a quote →

A temporary store or pop-up has to be ready in a few days and make a lasting impression: we design spaces that tell your brand's story, showcase the product and go up quickly, including in shopping centres and on high streets.

For permanent showrooms we create bespoke furniture, display walls, showcases and lighting using durable materials. Production and installation are in-house, so timing and quality stay under control.

What's included

  1. 01

    Retail concept

    Layout, customer journey and product display designed to sell.

  2. 02

    Temporary stores and pop-ups

    Lightweight structures that are quick to assemble, dismantle and reuse in other cities.

  3. 03

    Permanent showrooms

    Bespoke furniture, fitted walls, showcases and premium finishes.

  4. 04

    Shop-in-shop

    Brand corners inside stores and department stores, consistent with your image.

  5. 05

    Lighting and graphics

    Product lighting, signage, window graphics and print, all produced by us.

Why NEWSTANDS

  • Based in Milan

    We work from the Milan area, just a short drive from Fiera Milano Rho and Allianz MiCo: quick site visits, simple logistics and crews always close to the major exhibition centres.

  • In-house production

    Structures, wall panels, graphics, furniture and lighting are all made in our own workshop: fewer hand-offs, fewer surprises, deadlines met.

  • At every exhibition centre in Europe

    Our installation crews travel with your stand: Germany, France, Spain, Benelux, Austria, Switzerland and any other venue on request.

  • One point of contact

    From briefing to dismantling you always deal with the same person, plus a dedicated site manager at the show.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to fit out a temporary store?

It depends on the space and the project: thanks to in-house production we can install a pop-up in just a few days.

Can a pop-up be moved to other cities?

Yes, we design modular structures that can be dismantled and reassembled at other locations, in Italy and across Europe.

Do you also handle permanent showrooms?

Yes, from design through to the installation of furniture, showcases, lighting and graphics.

Got a trade fair coming up?

Tell us the trade fair, city, dates and floor space: we'll get back to you with an initial proposal and a clear quote.
