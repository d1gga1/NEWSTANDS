Service 07 / 08

Bespoke and rental stand furniture

Desks, counters, seating, display cases, kitchens and storerooms: furniture made to measure in our workshop or available to hire, for stands of every size.

Message us on WhatsAppRequest a quote →

Furniture is what makes a stand work: reception, display, meetings, tastings and storage all need to be in the right place. We design them together with the layout, with visitor flows and your team's work at the show in mind.

We can create one-off bespoke pieces in your brand's finishes and colours, or supply rental furniture to keep costs down. Delivery, installation and collection are always handled by us.

What's included

  1. 01

    Desks and reception

    Bespoke reception counters with logo, lighting and technical compartments.

  2. 02

    Meeting areas and lounges

    Tables, seating and lounge corners for meeting clients on your stand.

  3. 03

    Showcases and displays

    Display cases, shelving and fitted walls that show off your products.

  4. 04

    Kitchens and storerooms

    Storerooms, kitchens and service areas built into the stand.

  5. 05

    Furniture hire

    Rental furniture for every style and budget, delivered and collected by us.

Why NEWSTANDS

  • Based in Milan

    We work from the Milan area, just a short drive from Fiera Milano Rho and Allianz MiCo: quick site visits, simple logistics and crews always close to the major exhibition centres.

  • In-house production

    Structures, wall panels, graphics, furniture and lighting are all made in our own workshop: fewer hand-offs, fewer surprises, deadlines met.

  • At every exhibition centre in Europe

    Our installation crews travel with your stand: Germany, France, Spain, Benelux, Austria, Switzerland and any other venue on request.

  • One point of contact

    From briefing to dismantling you always deal with the same person, plus a dedicated site manager at the show.

Frequently asked questions

Can I hire just the furniture?

Yes, we also supply rental furniture on its own, with delivery to and collection from the show.

Can bespoke furniture be reused?

Yes, we keep it in our warehouse and bring it back for your next trade fair.

Do you deliver furniture abroad as well?

Yes, we deliver and install furniture at all the major exhibition centres in Europe.

Got a trade fair coming up?

Tell us the trade fair, city, dates and floor space: we'll get back to you with an initial proposal and a clear quote.
