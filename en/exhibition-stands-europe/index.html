From Milan to all of Europe

Exhibition stands across Europe

We start from Milan and travel to any exhibition centre in Europe: one supplier for design, production, transport and installation, wherever your trade fair is.

Message us on WhatsAppRequest a quote →

Exhibiting abroad with a different local stand builder in every country means new contacts, different standards and more risk. With NEWSTANDS you have a single team: we design and produce in our workshop near Milan, organise transport and access paperwork, and install with our own crews.

We work at the major exhibition centres in Germany, France, Spain, Benelux, Austria and Switzerland, and at any other venue on request. We have built national pavilions and brand stands, such as the Tunisia pavilion at MEDICA Düsseldorf.

Why NEWSTANDS

  • Based in Milan

    We work from the Milan area, just a short drive from Fiera Milano Rho and Allianz MiCo: quick site visits, simple logistics and crews always close to the major exhibition centres.

  • In-house production

    Structures, wall panels, graphics, furniture and lighting are all made in our own workshop: fewer hand-offs, fewer surprises, deadlines met.

  • At every exhibition centre in Europe

    Our installation crews travel with your stand: Germany, France, Spain, Benelux, Austria, Switzerland and any other venue on request.

  • One point of contact

    From briefing to dismantling you always deal with the same person, plus a dedicated site manager at the show.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle transport and paperwork for trade fairs abroad?

Yes: packing, transport, passes, safety documentation and liaison with the show organisers are all taken care of by us.

Are the installation crews your own?

Yes, your stand is installed by our own crews with a dedicated site manager, abroad too.

Which languages can we communicate in?

Italian and English; for show documentation we liaise with the organiser in the local language.

Got a trade fair coming up?

Tell us the trade fair, city, dates and floor space: we'll get back to you with an initial proposal and a clear quote.
