Exhibition stand installation and dismantling

Specialist crews and a dedicated site manager to install and dismantle your stand at Fiera Milano Rho, Allianz MiCo and every exhibition centre in Europe. Installation-only jobs too.

Message us on WhatsAppRequest a quote →

Installation is the moment a project becomes a stand: it takes experienced crews, the right equipment, and strict respect for time slots and the venue's safety rules. From our base in the Milan area we reach the Milan exhibition centres quickly and set off with your stand to trade fairs all over Europe.

We install stands we have produced ourselves as well as stands from other suppliers or owned by the client: receipt of materials, installation, final inspection, support during the show, dismantling and return to the warehouse.

What's included

  1. 01

    Specialist crews

    Experienced installers with their own equipment and a dedicated site manager.

  2. 02

    Access paperwork

    Passes, safety documentation and permits required by the exhibition centre.

  3. 03

    Inspection and handover

    Final check of structures, lighting and graphics before the show opens.

  4. 04

    On-site support

    Technicians on call throughout the show for anything you need.

  5. 05

    Dismantling and storage

    Dismantling on schedule, transport and storage for reuse.

Why NEWSTANDS

  • Based in Milan

    We work from the Milan area, just a short drive from Fiera Milano Rho and Allianz MiCo: quick site visits, simple logistics and crews always close to the major exhibition centres.

  • In-house production

    Structures, wall panels, graphics, furniture and lighting are all made in our own workshop: fewer hand-offs, fewer surprises, deadlines met.

  • At every exhibition centre in Europe

    Our installation crews travel with your stand: Germany, France, Spain, Benelux, Austria, Switzerland and any other venue on request.

  • One point of contact

    From briefing to dismantling you always deal with the same person, plus a dedicated site manager at the show.

Frequently asked questions

Do you install stands you didn't build?

Yes, we also install and dismantle stands from other stand builders or owned by the exhibitor.

Which exhibition centres do you work at?

Fiera Milano Rho and Allianz MiCo, Bologna, Verona and Rimini, and the major European hubs: Düsseldorf, Cologne, Frankfurt, Munich, Paris, Barcelona, Madrid, Amsterdam and beyond.

Do you comply with exhibition centre safety rules?

Yes, our installers work in accordance with the safety rules and technical regulations of every exhibition centre.

Got a trade fair coming up?

Tell us the trade fair, city, dates and floor space: we'll get back to you with an initial proposal and a clear quote.
