Modular, reusable stands

Modular systems designed for companies exhibiting at several shows a year: the same brand image, costs under control and fast installation, in Milan and at every exhibition centre in Europe.

Message us on WhatsAppRequest a quote →

A modular stand is a long-term investment: structures, panels and furniture can be reconfigured to suit different floor areas and stand types, from an inline stand to an island. The graphics change; the quality stays.

We design the system once, produce it in our workshop and manage it for you between shows: storage, maintenance, graphics updates and transport to the next exhibition hall, in Italy or abroad.

What's included

  1. 01

    A bespoke system

    Modules designed around your brand image, combinable for anything from a few square metres to large islands.

  2. 02

    Interchangeable graphics

    Printed fabrics and panels that can be swapped quickly for each show, product or market.

  3. 03

    Storage and maintenance

    We keep your stand in our warehouse and check it before every new departure.

  4. 04

    Fast installation

    Shorter build-up times, ideal even for shows with tight installation windows.

  5. 05

    Managed show calendar

    We plan your trade-fair season with you and organise logistics and crews for every stop.

Why NEWSTANDS

  • Based in Milan

    We work from the Milan area, just a short drive from Fiera Milano Rho and Allianz MiCo: quick site visits, simple logistics and crews always close to the major exhibition centres.

  • In-house production

    Structures, wall panels, graphics, furniture and lighting are all made in our own workshop: fewer hand-offs, fewer surprises, deadlines met.

  • At every exhibition centre in Europe

    Our installation crews travel with your stand: Germany, France, Spain, Benelux, Austria, Switzerland and any other venue on request.

  • One point of contact

    From briefing to dismantling you always deal with the same person, plus a dedicated site manager at the show.

Frequently asked questions

Is a modular stand cheaper than a custom one?

Over several shows it usually is: the initial investment is spread across each participation, and subsequent production costs come down to graphics and logistics.

Can a modular stand adapt to different floor areas?

Yes, the modules can be recombined for inline, corner, peninsula or island stands and for different floor areas.

Can you manage our whole trade-fair calendar?

Yes: from the Milan area we coordinate storage, transport and installation for every show of the season, in Italy and across Europe.

Got a trade fair coming up?

Tell us the trade fair, city, dates and floor space: we'll get back to you with an initial proposal and a clear quote.
