From Milan to all of Europe

Exhibition stands in Milan

Milan is one of Europe's most important trade-fair cities, and this is where we work from: we design, produce and install stands for shows at Fiera Milano Rho and Allianz MiCo, and for events across the city.

Message us on WhatsAppRequest a quote →

Every year Fiera Milano Rho hosts international shows such as Salone del Mobile.Milano, EICMA, HostMilano, TUTTOFOOD, MIDO and MICAM, while Allianz MiCo is one of the largest congress centres in Europe. For exhibitors, that means fierce competition: your stand has to stand out.

Having your stand builder in the Milan area means quick site visits, simple logistics, installation crews close by and immediate support during the show. And if your season continues abroad after Milan, the same team takes your stand to every exhibition centre in Europe.

What's included

  1. 01

    Fiera Milano Rho

    Stands for the major international shows at the Rho exhibition centre, from the design halls to motorcycles and food.

  2. 02

    Allianz MiCo

    Setups for congresses, conventions and trade fairs at Milan's congress centre.

  3. 03

    Events in the city

    Product launches, Fuorisalone, showrooms and temporary stores at venues across Milan.

  4. 04

    Quick site visits

    From our base we reach the Milan exhibition centres in no time, before and during the show.

  5. 05

    Storage close by

    Your stand stays in our warehouse in Lombardy, ready for the next edition.

Why NEWSTANDS

  • Based in Milan

    We work from the Milan area, just a short drive from Fiera Milano Rho and Allianz MiCo: quick site visits, simple logistics and crews always close to the major exhibition centres.

  • In-house production

    Structures, wall panels, graphics, furniture and lighting are all made in our own workshop: fewer hand-offs, fewer surprises, deadlines met.

  • At every exhibition centre in Europe

    Our installation crews travel with your stand: Germany, France, Spain, Benelux, Austria, Switzerland and any other venue on request.

  • One point of contact

    From briefing to dismantling you always deal with the same person, plus a dedicated site manager at the show.

Frequently asked questions

Are you a Milan-based stand builder?

Yes, we work from the Milan area, with our head office and workshop in Tromello (PV), Lombardy, a short drive from Fiera Milano Rho.

Which Milan trade fairs do you build stands for?

For every show at Fiera Milano Rho and Allianz MiCo, as well as for events and Fuorisalone in the city.

Can you also support us at trade fairs abroad?

Yes, from Milan we take stands and crews to all the major exhibition centres in Europe.

Got a trade fair coming up?

Tell us the trade fair, city, dates and floor space: we'll get back to you with an initial proposal and a clear quote.
