Custom exhibition stands

A one-off stand, built from scratch around your brand: we design it, produce it in our workshop near Milan and install it at any trade fair in Europe.

Message us on WhatsAppRequest a quote →

A custom stand is the strongest way to present yourself at a trade fair: every wall, every light and every visitor route is designed around your products and your sales goals. We start from the venue's technical regulations and the flow of the hall, then turn the idea into photorealistic 3D renders and construction drawings.

We produce timber and metal structures, wall panels, graphics, furniture and lighting in-house, so we control quality and timing at every stage. Whether the show is at Fiera Milano Rho, in Düsseldorf or in Paris, the crew that built your stand is the same crew that installs it.

What's included

  1. 01

    Concept and 3D renders

    Creative proposal, photorealistic renders and revisions until the stand is exactly the way you want it.

  2. 02

    Detailed design

    Technical drawings, calculations and documentation compliant with the regulations of every exhibition centre.

  3. 03

    Made-to-measure production

    Joinery, metalwork, glass, fabric and premium finishes produced in our own workshop.

  4. 04

    Graphics and lighting

    Large-format printing, lightboxes, LED walls and lighting design built into the stand's architecture.

  5. 05

    Installation and dismantling

    Transport, installation with a dedicated site manager, on-site support during the show, dismantling and storage.

Why NEWSTANDS

  • Based in Milan

    We work from the Milan area, just a short drive from Fiera Milano Rho and Allianz MiCo: quick site visits, simple logistics and crews always close to the major exhibition centres.

  • In-house production

    Structures, wall panels, graphics, furniture and lighting are all made in our own workshop: fewer hand-offs, fewer surprises, deadlines met.

  • At every exhibition centre in Europe

    Our installation crews travel with your stand: Germany, France, Spain, Benelux, Austria, Switzerland and any other venue on request.

  • One point of contact

    From briefing to dismantling you always deal with the same person, plus a dedicated site manager at the show.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build a custom stand?

It depends on size and complexity: for a bespoke stand it's best to start as soon as your participation in the show is confirmed, so there is time for design, renders and production.

Can a custom stand be reused?

Yes. We design the components to be stored and reassembled: for your next show we adapt the stand to a new floor area and new graphics.

Do you build custom stands outside Italy too?

Yes, from the Milan area we take your stand and our crews to all the major exhibition centres in Europe, from Germany to Spain.

Got a trade fair coming up?

Tell us the trade fair, city, dates and floor space: we'll get back to you with an initial proposal and a clear quote.
