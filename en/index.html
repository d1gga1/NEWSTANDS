Frequently askedquestions

§ 07 — FAQCosts, lead times, cities and services: answers to the questions we are asked most often before a trade fair.
01 / 06

How much does an exhibition stand cost?

The price of a stand depends on floor area, stand type (inline, corner, peninsula or island), materials, graphics, furniture and exhibition centre. After a short briefing on the show, dates and floor space, we send you an initial proposal and a clear, detailed quote. You can start with the 3D configurator above, or call us or message us on WhatsApp at +39 388 470 6887.

02 / 06

Where is NEWSTANDS based and which trade fairs do you work at?

We are an exhibition stand builder in the Milan area, with our head office and workshop in Tromello (PV), Lombardy: a strategic location just a short drive from Fiera Milano Rho and Allianz MiCo, which host some of the most important trade fairs in Europe. From here our crews travel to every exhibition centre: Bologna, Verona, Rimini, Düsseldorf, Cologne, Frankfurt, Munich, Paris, Lyon, Barcelona, Madrid, Amsterdam, Brussels, Vienna, Geneva and beyond.

03 / 06

What does the turnkey service include?

One point of contact for everything: briefing, concept and 3D renders, construction drawings, in-house production of structures, wall panels, large-format graphics, furniture and lighting, logistics and transport, installation with a dedicated site manager, support during the show, dismantling and storage for reuse.

04 / 06

Do you also handle events, congresses and showrooms?

Yes. As well as custom and modular exhibition stands, we design and build corporate events, congresses, conventions, product launches, stages and set designs, showrooms, temporary stores and pop-ups, complete with truss, LED walls and lighting.

05 / 06

How far in advance should we contact you?

The sooner you get in touch, the more time we have for design, renders and production: for a custom stand, it's best to start as soon as your participation in the show is confirmed. For modular stands and installation-only jobs we can also work to tighter deadlines: write to us and we'll check feasibility straight away.

06 / 06

How do I request a quote?

Fill in the form in the contact section, email info@newstandsallestimenti.com, or call us or message us on WhatsApp at +39 388 470 6887 (alternatively +39 388 388 6306). Tell us the trade fair or event, city, dates and floor space, and we'll get back to you with an initial proposal.

Got a trade faircoming up?

Tell us about the event, dates and floor space. We'll get back to you with an initial proposal and a clear quote.
