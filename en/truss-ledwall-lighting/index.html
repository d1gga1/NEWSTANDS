Truss, LED walls and lighting

Truss structures, suspended rings, rigging, LED walls and lighting for stands that can be seen from across the hall, at Fiera Milano and at every exhibition centre in Europe.

Message us on WhatsAppRequest a quote →

A suspended ring with your logo, an LED wall for product videos, lighting carefully designed for every surface: these are the elements that make a stand recognisable from a distance. We design them together with the stand, checking loads, hanging points and venue regulations.

We supply and install truss, motors, LED walls and lighting fixtures with qualified staff, coordinating rigging requests with the exhibition organiser.

What's included

  1. 01

    Truss structures

    Ground-supported and suspended structures of any size, also as a base for graphics and lighting.

  2. 02

    Rings and suspended signs

    Circular or custom hanging elements with graphics, visible from across the hall.

  3. 03

    Rigging

    Calculations, hanging points and paperwork with the exhibition centre.

  4. 04

    LED walls

    LED screens in a range of sizes, built into walls or mounted on dedicated structures.

  5. 05

    Lighting design

    Lighting plans for your products, graphics and the atmosphere of the stand.

Why NEWSTANDS

  • Based in Milan

    We work from the Milan area, just a short drive from Fiera Milano Rho and Allianz MiCo: quick site visits, simple logistics and crews always close to the major exhibition centres.

  • In-house production

    Structures, wall panels, graphics, furniture and lighting are all made in our own workshop: fewer hand-offs, fewer surprises, deadlines met.

  • At every exhibition centre in Europe

    Our installation crews travel with your stand: Germany, France, Spain, Benelux, Austria, Switzerland and any other venue on request.

  • One point of contact

    From briefing to dismantling you always deal with the same person, plus a dedicated site manager at the show.
